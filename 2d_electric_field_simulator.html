Mathematical Physics Equations

1. Coulomb's Law & Electric Field Vector

For $N$ point charges, the total field vector at position $\mathbf{r}$ is calculated via superposition:

E(r) = ∑ [ k · qi / |r - ri|2 ] · ûi
2. Electrostatic Force on Test Charge

The force exerted on a test charge $q_{test}$ at the probe position:

F = qtest · E
3. Electric Potential (Voltage)

Electric potential $V$ is a scalar sum of all charge contributions:

V(r) = ∑ [ k · qi / |r - ri| ]
